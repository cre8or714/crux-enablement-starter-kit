# Crux enablement starter kit, review

## Fluency tiers

- [P1] Ops analyst, sample: **Floor** (1 points)
    - uses AI daily (+1)
- [P2] Deal associate, sample: **Building** (3 points)
    - uses AI daily (+1)
    - has shipped a tool with AI help (+2)
- [P3] Senior engineer, sample: **Ceiling** (8 points)
    - uses AI daily (+1)
    - has shipped a tool with AI help (+2)
    - someone else actually uses it (+3)
    - has coached a colleague (+2)
- [P4] New hire, sample: **Floor** (0 points)

## Compliance gate

- [W1] Internal meeting notes summarizer, sample: **CLEARED**
    - no compliance triggers present
- [W2] Draft investor update email, sample: **NEEDS_REVIEW**
    - touches investor communications
    - touches trade or deal data
    - externally facing
- [W3] Internal onboarding FAQ bot, sample: **CLEARED**
    - no compliance triggers present
- [W4] Customer-facing deal status chatbot, sample: **NEEDS_REVIEW**
    - touches customer PII
    - touches trade or deal data
    - externally facing
